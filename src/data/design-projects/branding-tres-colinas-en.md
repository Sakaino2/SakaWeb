## Project Summary

Branding project for a small company dedicated to the agricultural sector.

### Features

- Visual identity creation.
- Typography and color selection.
- Creation of stationery for presentations and packaging.

### Design Process

The project foundations were discussed with the client, seeking an identity that reflects a feature of the farm where the company is located, which has three hills, and also aiming to highlight the natural image in the design.
