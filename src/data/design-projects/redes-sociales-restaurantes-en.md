## Project Summary

Some of the pieces designed during my work at Wasabi S.A.C. for the various restaurants managed by the company.

### Features

- Design of advertising pieces for social media.
- Editing of videos for events, promotions and other purposes.
- Visual identity design for various restaurants.

### Design Process

Establishing a brand identity for each restaurant and keeping it consistent in every piece.

### Learnings

Designing a variety of brand identities for advertising pieces that represent each restaurant's characteristics, since being different types of cuisine, they required a different visual line.
